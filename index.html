Learn ML Strategy with Phoebe

Most machine learning teams do not lose months to the wrong model. They lose them to the wrong next step: a dev set that cannot see the improvement, a metric nobody can optimise, a fascinating project with a small ceiling funded ahead of a boring one with a large ceiling, and a dev set that got memorised because everybody looked at all of it. Andrew Ng wrote the discipline down in 58 short chapters. This course rebuilds it around one classifier: Lanternfish's trail-camera species model, 1,000 dev images, 120 wrong. Six leader sessions on the decisions, ten practitioner sessions on the mechanics, and a bench where you choose the order of work and watch both halves of the dev set answer.

Practitioner track 🔧 · ten sessions, one decision

One classifier, Lanternfish's species model, taken from a dev set that tells the truth to a scored order of work. Session 10 runs the whole decision on the bench.

🎯 Practitioner session 1 · start here

Dev and test sets that tell the truth

Same distribution as the traffic you care about, sized for the difference you need to see: 100 examples cannot detect 0.1 percent, 10,000 can.

▶ Start here ⚡ Practitioner session 2

The first system in a week

Build quickly, then iterate. The 12 percent baseline and why waiting for the right architecture costs more than shipping the wrong one.

 📋 Practitioner session 3

The error-analysis spreadsheet

Sixty-four real errors, five categories, a share column and a ceiling column. The order of work falls out of it.

 👁️ Practitioner session 4

Eyeball and Blackbox dev sets

Ten percent you look at, ninety you never do. Hand rules against what you saw take one half to 3.8 percent and leave the other at 11.2.

 📐 Practitioner session 5

Bias and variance from three numbers

Train 3, dev 12, human-level 2: avoidable bias 1, variance 9. Which project each diagnosis funds.

 📈 Practitioner session 6

Learning curves, read honestly

Plotting training and dev error against data size, and the two shapes that tell you more data will or will not help.

 🌍 Practitioner session 7

Training and testing on different distributions

When it is right, the training-dev set that separates variance from mismatch, and what to do about mismatch.

 🔬 Practitioner session 8

The Optimization Verification test

Is the model scoring wrong, or the search failing to find the answer it would score highest? One test tells them apart.

 🧩 Practitioner session 9

Error analysis by parts

A detector then a classifier. Perfect inputs to each stage, and the error that belongs to only one of them.

 🎛️ Practitioner session 10

The what-to-fix-next bench

Five projects, two halves of a dev set, your order. Points per week against the interesting project, and the rules that only fix what you looked at.
